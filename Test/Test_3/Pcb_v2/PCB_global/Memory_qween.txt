Power Topology & Distribution
True Distributed DC Bus: Rugged, shared high-voltage DC bus (12V-18V). Local Point-of-Load (PoL) 3.3V/5V buck converters on every node.
Active PowerPath Load Sharing: Reject simple ideal diodes for source OR-ing. Mandate Active PowerPath Controllers (e.g., LTC4370) to seamlessly blend Solar and Battery currents during heavy load transients, preventing UVLO brownouts.
Hysteretic Hardware Shedding: Hardware load shedding must use dedicated UVLO ICs with built-in hysteresis and soft-start to prevent high-frequency chatter/oscillation during battery ESR sags.
Communication, Grounding & Physical Layer
Single Continuous Ground Plane: Solid, unbroken ground plane per node.
Integrated Galvanic Isolation (Option B Mandate): Every node uses Integrated Signal & Power Isolators (e.g., TI ISOW1412) to provide full galvanic isolation without the footprint penalty of bulky DC-DC modules.
6-Pin Isolated Backbone: V_BUS, GND_POWER, CAN_H, CAN_L, CAN_GND (isolated secondary reference), and SHIELD (hybrid terminated via 1nF-10nF capacitor).
Ruggedized Connectors: M12 or Molex Micro-Fit 3.0. Strict Hot-Plug Power-Good sequencing to prevent CAN backfeeding.
