# Phase-1 Intelligent Power Node

# Architecture Freeze Report v0.1

Status: ARCHITECTURE PARTIALLY FROZEN

Date: June 2026

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the currently frozen architecture decisions for the Phase-1 Intelligent Power Node.

The purpose of this board is to:

* Harvest solar energy.
* Maintain a 12V SLA battery.
* Provide regulated power rails.
* Collect telemetry.
* Operate autonomously for extended periods.
* Serve as the foundation of a future distributed intelligent energy network.

This document intentionally freezes only architectural decisions.

Component-level selections remain open unless explicitly stated.

---

# 2. Design Philosophy

Priority Order:

1. Safety
2. Reliability
3. Simplicity
4. Manufacturability
5. Cost
6. Efficiency
7. Compactness

The system shall prefer proven hardware solutions over firmware-controlled safety mechanisms.

Battery safety must never depend on software.

The MCU shall function as an observer rather than a safety controller.

---

# 3. System Inputs

## Solar Source

Loom Solar Panel

* Maximum Power: 10W
* Vmp: 18.8V
* Imp: 0.56A
* Voc: 22.6V
* Isc: 0.60A

## Energy Storage

12V Sealed Lead Acid Battery

* Capacity: 7Ah
* Energy: ~84Wh
* Float Voltage: 13.6V–13.8V
* Cyclic Voltage: 14.6V–14.8V

# 4. Mandatory Operating Modes

The architecture must support all of the following modes:

## Mode A

Solar Present

Battery Present

System Operational

## Mode B

Solar Present

Battery Absent

System Operational

## Mode C

Solar Absent

Battery Present

System Operational

## Mode D

Solar Present

Battery Removed During Operation

System Remains Operational

Any future architecture that fails any of these operating modes shall be rejected.

---

# 5. Architecture Principles

The system shall be separated into three logical domains:

## Energy Harvesting Domain

Responsible for extracting energy from the solar panel.

Responsibilities:

* Solar protection
* Solar regulation
* Battery charging

## Energy Storage Domain

Responsible for:

* Battery charging
* Battery maintenance
* Battery monitoring

## System Power Domain

Responsible for:

* Delivering regulated power to system loads
* Supporting uninterrupted operation
* Allowing operation from solar, battery, or both

This separation is considered a frozen architectural principle.

# 6. Power Architecture Status

Current Architecture Candidate:

Solar Panel
→ Protection
→ Charging Subsystem
→ SLA Battery

AND

Solar Source + Battery Source
→ Power Path Subsystem
→ 12V System Bus

The exact implementation remains under investigation.

The architecture must guarantee:

* Solar-only operation
* Battery-only operation
* Solar + battery operation
* Battery removal survival

The exact charger and power-path implementation are NOT frozen.

# 7. Power Rail Architecture

Frozen.

The board shall generate:

## Rail 1

12V System Bus

Purpose:

Primary system power distribution.

## Rail 2

5V Rail

Generated from the 12V system bus.

Purpose:

Peripheral and future expansion power.

## Rail 3

3.3V Rail

Generated from the 5V rail.

Purpose:

MCU
Sensors
Telemetry circuitry
Future communication modules

Current preferred topology:

12V Bus
→ 5V Buck Converter
→ 3.3V LDO

Component selection remains open.

# 9. Safety Architecture

Frozen.

Battery safety shall be implemented in hardware.

The MCU shall not perform:

* Charge control
* Float regulation
* Bulk charge regulation
* Current limiting
* Safety shutdown decisions

Hardware subsystems shall remain safe even if the MCU is absent, crashed, or unpowered.

---

# 10. Sensor Architecture

Frozen.

## Current/Voltage Monitoring

Preferred solution:

INA226-class power monitors.

Purpose:

* Solar monitoring
* Battery monitoring

## Temperature Monitoring

Preferred solution:

10k NTC thermistor.

Purpose:

Battery temperature monitoring.

Exact part numbers remain open.

---

# 11. Communications Architecture

Frozen.

Phase-1 Power Node shall not contain mandatory CAN or CAN-FD hardware.

The node shall instead expose expansion interfaces.

Required interfaces:

* UART
* I²C
* SPI
* GPIO
* 3.3V
* 5V
* GND

Future communication technologies may be added through expansion modules.
