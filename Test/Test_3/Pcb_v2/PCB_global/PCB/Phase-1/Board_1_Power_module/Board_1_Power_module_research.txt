# Phase-1 Intelligent Power Node

# Architecture & Power Policy Freeze v1.0

Status: FROZEN

Date: June 2026

---

# 1. Purpose

The Phase-1 Intelligent Power Node is the foundational energy-management node of the distributed energy network.

The node is responsible for:

* Harvesting solar energy.
* Managing battery energy storage.
* Supplying regulated system power.
* Monitoring energy flow and system health.
* Operating autonomously for long durations.
* Providing reliable telemetry for future network integration.

This board is not designed as a simple charger.

This board is an Energy Management Node.

---

# 2. Core Design Philosophy

The design priorities are frozen in the following order:

1. Safety
2. Reliability
3. Autonomous Operation
4. Manufacturability
5. Serviceability
6. Efficiency
7. Cost
8. Compactness

No future design decision may violate a higher-priority objective to optimize a lower-priority objective.

---

# 3. System Philosophy

The node shall be treated as an energy-management system operating under a constrained power budget.

The node must continuously balance:

* Energy generation
* Energy storage
* Energy consumption

The architecture shall always prioritize maintaining node operation before maximizing battery charging speed.

---

# 4. Hardware Safety Philosophy

Battery safety shall be implemented entirely in hardware.

The MCU shall not be responsible for:

* Charge current regulation
* Charge voltage regulation
* Overcharge protection
* Reverse-current protection
* Battery disconnect protection
* Safety-critical power switching

The system must remain electrically safe if the MCU crashes, resets, locks up, or is physically removed.

# 7. Energy Arbitration Policy

Status: FROZEN

The node shall operate according to the following priority hierarchy.

Priority 1:
Maintain System Operation

Priority 2:
Maintain Battery Health

Priority 3:
Maximize Battery Charging

Therefore:

Load Priority > Battery Priority > Charging Speed

---

# 8. Energy Allocation Rules

## Rule 1

When solar power exceeds load demand:

Solar powers the system.

Remaining power charges the battery.

## Rule 2

When solar power is insufficient:

Solar contributes available power.

Battery supplies the deficit.

## Rule 3

When battery reaches low voltage threshold:

Battery charging behavior may be modified.

System operation remains prioritized.

## Rule 4

When battery reaches critical protection threshold:

Non-essential loads may be disconnected.

Battery protection takes precedence.

## Rule 5

When battery is absent:

Solar shall directly power the system bus.

## Rule 6

When solar is absent:

Battery shall power the system bus.
